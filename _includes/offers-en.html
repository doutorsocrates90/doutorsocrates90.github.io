How I can help

1

Innovation and management strategy

For creative businesses, collectives and civil-society organisations with good work but no shared plan, numbers or workflow.

  • Diagnostic: SWOT/TOWS and the degree of innovation, measured with the Innovation Radar
  • Mission, objectives and revenue targets for each product or programme
  • A management model: roles, processes, task and finance tools
  • Indicators to track and report results

Proof: Casa Criatura strategic plan; applied research with 33 cultural organisations in Pernambuco.

2

Your own ERP, on your own server

For small teams that outgrew spreadsheets and chat apps, want to own their data and cannot pay per-user licences.

  • Needs workshops and a clear map of what is native, community or custom
  • An open-source ERP of your own, set up in your language: projects, finance, contracts, approvals, people
  • Custom workflows, such as minutes that become tasks and expense batches read by OCR
  • Hosting on your own server, backups and a user manual your team keeps

Proof: LabHacker Maceió and GIG.

3

Funding, research and partnerships

For organisations and networks looking for grants, consortia or investment.

  • Funding strategy and proposal writing, from national calls to EU and international programmes
  • Research and reports for funders and policy audiences
  • Partnership and network facilitation across countries
  • Independent review and assessment of proposals and programmes

Proof: EU-funded CoAct and Critical Making reports; prize assessment; GIG.

Also available: workshops and courses on innovation measurement and open education.
