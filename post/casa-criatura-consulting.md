Casa Criatura is a creation and innovation laboratory in Olinda's historic site: a house of architects, artists and designers working with digital fabrication, a co-working space, a gallery and, since 2019, a Latin American node of the [Careables](/post/careables-latin-america.html) network. When the pandemic hit in 2020 and the team began producing open-source face shields and aerosol boxes, the house needed more than ideas: it needed a business model, a workflow and numbers.

## What we built together

**Strategy Criatura (June to December 2020).** A full strategic plan for the collective:

- Mission, vision and values, and annual objectives with revenue targets per product line
- SWOT/TOWS diagnosis and a strategy matrix (attack, improve, maintain, "careful, it may explode")
- Product axes with customers, value proposition, revenue, costs and delivery checklist for each: **Education** (online courses), **Health** (Careables and open-source medical items), **Design** (fashion and furniture) and **Co-working** (space rental)
- Management tooling: Scrum sprints, Trello and Slack for tasks, shared documents, and a simple financial control model
- Marketing and sales notes, plus a risk and mitigation table and indicators to evaluate each product (items made, donated and sold; audience feedback; revenue per line)

**Planning 2021 to 2026.** A second cycle that refined the purpose, mission and vision around local productive arrangements and a creative chain that answers real needs, and prepared project documents for partners and funders.

**Digital presence.** Scoping of an online shop and an open-source repository of downloadable products with an interactive 3D viewer, so that designs could be shared, tracked and sold.

## Why it mattered

The work translated a creative collective's instincts into a plan that could be read by funders, partners and the team itself, and linked Casa Criatura's local production to international networks such as the Global Innovation Gathering and Careables.
