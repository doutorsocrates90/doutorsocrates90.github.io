What it does

  • Vaults with a role per member: read, edit or manage
  • Passwords stay hidden: Reveal shows one for 30 seconds, Copy sends it to the clipboard
  • Access log with user, time and IP, strength check and change-by dates

Screenshots

Before you store passwords

Passwords are stored in plain text in the database; they are not encrypted. Anyone with access to the database, a backup or the server shell can read them, so encrypt your backups and keep your most critical secrets (database, root or banking passwords) in a dedicated password manager. The password field is not returned by normal reads, searches or exports, and only members of a vault can reveal or copy its passwords.
