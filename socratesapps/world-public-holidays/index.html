World Public Holidays

Pick a country, get its holidays, automatically

Get it on Odoo Apps · Odoo 19 ↗Odoo 18 ↗Odoo 20 ↗€ 19

What it does

  • About one hundred countries, with regions and states
  • A weekly job adds each new year on its own
  • Nothing is fetched until you allow it; only country code and year are sent

Screenshots

Requirements

Odoo 18.0, 19.0 or 20.0 Community. The server needs outbound HTTPS access to date.nager.at, only when you ask for holidays.
